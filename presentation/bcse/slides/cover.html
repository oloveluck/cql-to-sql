Breast cancer screening, evaluated

One quality measure, computed two ways: by a rules engine reading health records, and as a database query

One measure · two methods · three test populations

This talk follows one clinical quality measure end to end: what it asks, how it is published as rules a computer can run, the two ways we computed it, the test data, and what came out. Every result is labelled with the data it came from; slide 7 puts them all side by side.
