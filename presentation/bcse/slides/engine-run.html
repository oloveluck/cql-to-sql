Method A is how quality measures are usually computed. A rules engine takes NCQA's published rules and code lists, prepares them once, then goes through the members one at a time: it reads each member's health records and answers the four checks. Adding up the answers gives the counts and the rate, written as a standard FHIR results report. Our engine runs this way in memory, and also behind our own FHIR server.
