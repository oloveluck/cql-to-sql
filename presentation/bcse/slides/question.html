The measure

“Of the women who should be screened for breast cancer, how many were?”

1 · Eligible?

A woman aged 42–74, continuously enrolled with medical coverage

2 · Excused?

Bilateral mastectomy, hospice, palliative care, death, or 66+ and frail

3 · Accountable

Eligible and not excused: the denominator

4 · Screened?

A mammogram from Oct 2023 to Dec 2025: the numerator

Rate = numerator ÷ denominator. US health plans report it every year for NCQA’s HEDIS program.

BCS-E is NCQA's Breast Cancer Screening measure in its electronic, FHIR-based form. Every member goes through the same four checks. It judges the health plan, not the patient: of the people the plan is accountable for, what share got the care.
