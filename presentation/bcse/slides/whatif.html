A what-if that mirrors how health plans send clinical data to NCQA; not NCQA’s rule. The real fix is in the data: record screenings on a claim or as a test result.

To show the gap comes from how the data is recorded, not from the rules, we ran a what-if: each mammogram in the clinical record was also sent as a test result, which is how health plans pass clinical data to NCQA. The rate goes from 36.1% to 60.4%, against 60.9% expected, and 983 of the 1,000 members match the key. The default stays faithful to the data as delivered.
