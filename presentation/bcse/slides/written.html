Which billing and clinical codes count: “mammogram” alone is 162 codes

The “screened?” rule, as NCQA wrote it

define "Numerator":
exists("Observations for mammography")
or exists("Reports for mammography")
or exists("Claims for mammography")
or exists("Eobs for mammography")

A mammogram in a test result, an imaging report, a claim or a claim payment.

Not on the list: a procedure entry in the clinical record.

NCQA publishes the measure as computable rules in a standard format, so any compliant system can run it. The rules are readable: the screening rule looks for a mammogram in four kinds of record. It never looks at procedure entries in the clinical record. Keep that in mind; it explains most of the results later.
